Use cases

Eight capabilities in three steps: know, find, keep. Each page explains where the work gets hard, how HunterBuddy handles it, and what is still yours to judge.

  1. 01 · Know Industry primer: from an unfamiliar sector to a confident first call A new sector means a pile of jargon, a value chain you can’t see clearly and a JD full of insider terms. Read more
  2. 02 · Know Client research: decide if it’s worth pursuing, then how to open Some clients have plenty of openings, and only after chasing them do you find the need, the budget or the decision chain isn’t ready. Read more
  3. 03 · Know Org mapping: from a group of people to an org chart Mapping means piecing things together from websites and the news, and the finished chart lives on in several versions. Read more
  4. 04 · Find Multi-source sourcing: scattered leads, gathered into one list Candidates are spread across recruiting platforms, papers, code repositories and your own database. One pass through them takes a whole day. Read more
  5. 05 · Find Identity checks: from one ID to one real person All you have is an alias and a GitHub ID. Working out who the person really is can take half a day of guesswork. Read more
  6. 06 · Find Two-way job matching: CVs find roles, roles find CVs Thousands of CVs sit in your database, and the best fit often never turns up in a search. Read more
  7. 07 · Keep Referrals: who is most likely to know them Who comes to mind for a referral depends on your memory, not on who really knows the person. Read more
  8. 08 · Keep Talent pool: one profile per person, kept current The people you spoke with years ago have changed companies and fields, and your database is still stuck on the day they were added. Read more
  9. 09 What does a headhunter do? Daily work, challenges and AI A client on one side, candidates on the other: what fills a headhunter’s day. Read more
  10. 10 AI assistant for headhunters: what it is, what AI agents do What an AI assistant for headhunters can and can’t do, and how to choose one. Read more
